# Fact-check notes from the source film (pattern to follow)

Every checkable claim gets a row in the film's `NOTES.md`: time, claim, status, real source, treatment. These were
the decisions on the DeepSeek film (checked Oct 21-24, 2026).

| Claim (his words) | Status | Treatment on screen |
|---|---|---|
| "On October 20th, the US government ... banning DeepSeek from the federal system" | accurate: a DHS order dated Oct 20, 2026 (Federal Register) barring DeepSeek in federal civilian agencies, reported by UPI, CNBC, NYT | dated news card OCT 20 2026, accurate paraphrase, no outlet logos, no quoted order text |
| "Homeland Security says it could send the sensitive data to China" | DHS's stated concern | "THEIR WORRY: DATA -> CHINA", "could be sent" (a worry, not a proven leak) |
| "South Korea, Italy, and Germany is also restricting it" | restrictions reported in those countries | map pins + "NOT JUST THE US" list, RESTRICTING stamp |
| "the US put 100% tariff on BYD cars" | accurate in substance: Section 301 tariffs on Chinese EVs of 100%+ since Sept 2024 | his words stay; sourced chip "US tariff on Chinese EVs: 100%+" (BYD cars are not sold in the US: OK) |
| Enigma "started sending that data back to Germany" | hypothetical analogy, not history | the diorama is stamped **WHAT IF?** |
| "when the war starts, if there is a war ... depend on someone's else AI" | hypothetical | stamped **IF THERE IS A WAR**; unit blocks only, non-graphic |
| "security threat and a power struggle", the verdict, "competition always stays healthy" | opinion | labelled MADHAV'S TAKE / MADHAV'S VERDICT / "- Madhav's take" |
| "India wouldn't allow Starlink" (first take) | wrong: India licensed Starlink in 2025 | cut from the spine before building (an approved EDL cut, never a rewrite of his voice) |

Rules that came out of it: no disclaimers or "not endorsed" lines; never put an unverified number on screen;
replace real seals and logos with plain drawn badges; label analogies and hypotheticals on screen.
