# Hook choices (frame 0 = the cover, held ~2.5 s)

How the source film's hook evolved, and why:

| Version | Frame-0 headline | Why it changed |
|---|---|---|
| v1 | open case file, stamped "THE US JUST / BANNED / DEEPSEEK" + a Federal Register-style notice with ripping redaction bars | busy; the notice was readable for only ~1 s |
| v2 | red band "BREAKING · OCT 20" + "The US just banned DeepSeek from federal systems", BANNED stamp on the whale photo | trending-hook rule: the news itself, specific, in one breath, with the date; show the actual thing |
| v3 | "BREAKING · OCT 20 / The US just banned DeepSeek. Was it the right call?" | adds the question the video answers |
| v5-v7 | "US NEWS · OCT 20 / The US just banned / a Chinese AI. / Was that fair?" | his re-recorded spoken hook, exactly: the headline must match his first sentence word for word |

Pattern: a red strip label with the date, a 2-3 line Anton headline with a highlighter swipe on the verb, the
question in red, and the subject's photo in the column getting a stamp on the key word. Since v6 it sits under the
series tag "MICROSOFT ENGINEER ANSWERS YOUR IG QUESTIONS".
