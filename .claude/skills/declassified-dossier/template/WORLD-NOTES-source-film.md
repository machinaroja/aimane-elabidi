# c1-deepseek — "Declassified Dossier" world notes (checkpoint)

## Concept
A manila case file opens on a dark charcoal desk under a warm desk lamp (soft vignette, warm light pool).
Typewritten memo pages, rubber stamps (CLASSIFIED, BANNED), black redaction bars that slide over words and
rip away, paper clips, red string, a Federal Register-style notice, a paper map with a red US/China fault
line, a red-pencil "threat vectors" diagram. Documents are *dealt* onto the desk (slide in, settle, slide
off). His footage hands off into / out of paper-clipped photos in the file (beat `tile`).

## Palette / type
- manila #d8c08a, desk charcoal #1c1a18/#2a2622, ink #16130f, stamp red #c0282d, highlighter #f5e34a,
  memo paper #f3ead6, lamp warm #ffcf7a.
- Typewriter mono: Courier Prime (700) for documents + captions. Headlines: Anton (heavy condensed).
- Textures: `src/world/tex/*.jpg` generated by `src/world/make_textures.py` (desk, manila, memo).

## Hero object
The DEEPSEEK case file (manila folder, red CLASSIFIED stamp, paper-clipped whale photo). Opens at frame 0,
closes in the END card with a final stamp.
