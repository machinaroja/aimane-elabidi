# Hook / headline choices across versions (frame 0 = the cover)

| Version | Frame-0 headline | Why it changed |
|---|---|---|
| v1 | "IS THE AI BOOM A BUBBLE?" over his full frame + a small bubble top-right | first build |
| v2 | "Is the AI boom a bubble? A Microsoft engineer's take" | brief: trending AI-news Reels open with the specific thing + a concrete noun, in one breath, as a big headline |
| v3 | "Is the AI boom a bubble? Here's what I see at Microsoft." (glass card in the top band, coins already pouring at frame 0) | faster pacing; promise a first-hand view |
| v5 / v6 (final) | **"I barely write code anymore. Here's what I do instead."** (glass card under the series tag, "write code" in the iridescent gradient, "instead." in gold; bubble + city in the right column) | his re-recorded take opens with that line; rule: headline = his spoken hook, exactly |

Series tag from v6: "MICROSOFT ENGINEER ANSWERS YOUR IG QUESTIONS" (frame 0 to the end).
End title: "Is the AI boom a bubble?" + "Follow for more AI news" + handle.

Lesson: the personal, specific claim ("I barely write code anymore") is a stronger opener than the generic question
("Is the AI boom a bubble?"); keep the generic question for the IG question card, the comment prompt and the end card.
