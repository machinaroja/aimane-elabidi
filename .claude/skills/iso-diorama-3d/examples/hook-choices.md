- * "Robots will take these jobs first. Is yours on the list?"

before the number was dropped, because the v5 take no longer lists exactly five).

butter, Fredoka 74 px with a plum extrusion) + a coral pill "Is yours on the list?", over the island,
under the series tag. The end card turns it into the question "WHICH JOBS WILL ROBOTS TAKE FIRST?".

Lessons: the headline must be his exact spoken hook; drop numbers the final cut does not deliver;
keep it to two lines so the island stays visible on the cover.
