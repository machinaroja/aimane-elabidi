# News batch v5: recording script (7 reels, "I read the question, then I answer")

> Real example (Oct 23, 2026). Madhav recorded reels 1-5 from this; the Xbox and OpenAI ones were not re-recorded. Later fact checks cut more lines (see fact-check-log.md).

**Format for every reel (45-60 s):**
1. **HOOK, 0-3 s.** Say it to the camera, punchy, about 8 words. Pick one of the options; ⭐ = my pick.
2. **Q1.** Read the question out loud as if someone asked you. It's fine to glance at your phone.
   Pause 1 s, then answer from the talking points in your own words. Don't read the answers.
3. **Q2.** Same again.
4. **ASK.** Put the question to viewers (it drives comments), then "Follow for more AI news."

**Why some words are bold:** the existing animations trigger on them. Say each **bold** word or phrase somewhere in that
section and the visuals will line up with your new take automatically. Everything around them is up to you.
