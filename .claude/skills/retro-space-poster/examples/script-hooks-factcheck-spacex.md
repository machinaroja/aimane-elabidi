# Example: script, hook choices and fact-check (SpaceX news Reel)

## Hook options offered before recording
- (chosen) "Seattle to Delhi in one hour. On a rocket."
- "SpaceX is about to do something no one has done."
- "Rockets are about to get as cheap as flights."

Why the chosen one won: a concrete, personal picture (his own route, a number) in one breath. On screen
it is printed exactly as spoken, as the frame-0 cover.

## Final script (his re-recorded take, every word his own voice)
1. Seattle to Delhi in one hour on a rocket? Hell yeah, I will ride that.
2. (reads the IG question) What is SpaceX trying to do?
3. Imagine a rocket, if you can reuse it as a plane and you don't have to throw the plane away when you
   reach your destination, isn't that the best thing?
4. You take your car out from your home, you go to a grocery store, you don't leave your car there. You
   have to bring it back home and then go back tomorrow for the grocery shopping again, right? Imagine you
   have to buy a car every time you go to the grocery shopping. It doesn't make sense.
5. What they're trying to do is, they're building the best reusable rocket.
6. If they can do that, you can go to Seattle to New Delhi, New Delhi to Mexico in less than an hour.
7. And I think in 10 years, everything will be like that. That's what I would say.
End card (silent): "Would you ride one? Comment below." / "Follow for more AI news".

## Fact-check notes and treatments
| Claim | Status | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| SpaceX will try to catch both Starship stages on the next flight (Flight 15) | accurate per SpaceX's Q3 update (Oct 2026) | chip PLAN: CATCH BOTH STAGES; chip NEXT UP STARSHIP FLIGHT 15 · TARGETED FOR NOVEMBER 2026 |
| Seattle to Delhi in under an hour by rocket | SpaceX's Earth-to-Earth idea is a concept, not built | chip REALITY CHECK: EARTH-TO-EARTH ROCKET TRAVEL IS STILL A CONCEPT, held to the end card |
| "In 10 years, everything will be like that" | his prediction | badge MADHAV'S PREDICTION |
| "the best thing" | his opinion | chip MY TAKE: BEST THING! |

No disclaimer lines. If the flight happens before posting, change the question/answer, don't patch it
with a caption.
